# Concept figure color improvements

Styling-only improvement of eleven concept diagrams and four analytical teaching plots. The common palette uses blue for process/data and physical relations, teal for feasible/checked states and audits, orange for raw/unverified states or failure branches, and purple for statistical structure or alternative projections. Labels, line styles, hatching and node shapes retain monochrome interpretability. Measured result figures and numerical data are not modified.
